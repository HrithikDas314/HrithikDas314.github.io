About

I come from a non-CS background and taught myself the path into AI: deep learning, retrieval-augmented generation and web development. My thesis, JuteRAG, takes a photo of a jute leaf, finds the disease, and writes an advisory in Bangla using trusted agricultural sources.


I care about systems that are honest about their limits, which is why I measure how faithful the generated answers are to their sources instead of only reporting model accuracy. I am currently preparing a journal extension of my thesis work and learning more through a University of Tokyo online AI course.
