About

I am an IoT and Robotics Engineering graduate from the University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh, with a strong interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent software systems.


My undergraduate thesis was an AI-based jute leaf disease detection and advisory system that combines deep learning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and large language models. I have also built IoT and machine learning systems for agriculture and climate forecasting, and I co-authored a paper presented at IEEE SPICSCON 2025.


Beyond engineering, I have led a student organization, helped organize national-level events and worked on field data collection for UNDP-backed youth programmes.
